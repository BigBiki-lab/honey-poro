Draw and label a diagram of a metal lattice.
Explain why electrons are able to move freely through the structure of a metal.

Metals consist of positive ions in a regular lattice.

Outer-shell electrons become delocalised.

Delocalised electrons can move throughout the metal.

Their movement allows metals to conduct electricity.

Explain why electrons do not normally leave the surface of a metal.

Electrons are attracted to the positive metal ions by electrostatic forces. This attraction keeps the electrons within the metal.
